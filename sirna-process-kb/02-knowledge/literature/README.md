## Why the template insists on scale

Most published oligonucleotide process work is done at analytical or small preparative
scale. Most of the difficulty in a commercial process appears only at large scale: packing
quality at diameter, thermal uniformity in a large bed or vessel, blend time, membrane
performance at high concentration. A literature note that records a result without recording
the scale it was obtained at will eventually be used to justify a design decision it cannot
support.
