# Scope and charter

## Purpose

To hold, in one place, the process and facility knowledge required to design, build,
qualify, and run a greenfield cGMP manufacturing facility for siRNA drug substance.

## In scope

- siRNA drug substance, from raw material receipt to drug substance release.
- Both single-strand trains plus annealing to duplex.
- Blockmer preparation and, where the route uses it, enzymatic ligation.
- All downstream unit operations, equipment, buffers, reagents, and process conditions.
- PAT and analytical strategy supporting the above.
- Facility, utility, and system requirements arising from the process.
- Quality and regulatory strategy for the drug substance.

## Out of scope unless explicitly added

- Drug product formulation, including lipid nanoparticle and conjugate-specific
  fill-finish. **[ASSUMED]**
- Non-clinical and clinical development.
- Commercial supply chain and distribution.
- Business case and financial modelling, except where process choices drive cost of goods.

## Intended audiences

| Audience | What they take from here |
|---|---|
| Process engineering | Unit operation definitions, mass balance, scaling rules |
| Facility and capital project | URS, BoD input, area classification, utility loads |
| Analytical development | Method register, PAT strategy, IPC requirements |
| Quality and regulatory | Control strategy, CQA to CPP linkage, validation approach |
| Manufacturing | Process conditions, failure modes, recovery actions |

## Route decision

The knowledge base deliberately carries **both** the fully chemical solid-phase route and
the chemoenzymatic blockmer plus ligation route until the route decision is taken and
recorded as a decision record. See `10-decisions/decision-log.md`.

## Governance

Changes are made by pull request. Baselined entries require a named reviewer. The
registers in `04-materials/`, `05-equipment/`, `09-risk/`, `10-decisions/`, and
`11-open-questions/` are the controlled artifacts and take precedence over prose.
