# Validation approach

> **[SEED]**. Skeleton of the Validation Master Plan.

## Lifecycle framing

| Stage | Activity | Source in this knowledge base |
|---|---|---|
| Process design | Define the commercial process from development knowledge | `02-knowledge/`, `03-process/` |
| Process qualification | Demonstrate the process performs reproducibly at scale | PPQ protocol, drawn from UO entries |
| Continued process verification | Ongoing assurance during routine production | Control strategy, PAT |

## Facility and equipment

Follow ASTM E2500 risk-based verification: requirements in the URS set, verification scaled
to risk, with subject matter expert approval. Avoid the older habit of testing every
requirement at equal depth, which consumes schedule without improving assurance.

Commissioning and qualification sequence: design review, factory acceptance, site acceptance,
installation and operational verification, performance verification.

## Computerised systems

GAMP 5 categorisation per system, with validation effort matched to category and to GxP
impact. Data integrity requirements apply to every system generating GMP records, including
chromatography data systems and the lyophiliser control system.

## Process qualification

Number of PPQ batches should be justified by risk and by process understanding rather than
set at a conventional number. **[SEED]** Areas of highest residual uncertainty, principally
chromatography resolution at commercial scale and annealing uniformity, deserve the most
qualification attention.

## Cleaning validation

Scope depends on the multi-product decision, OQ-009. Carryover limits, sampling methods,
and worst-case product selection must be defined. Chromatography resin cleaning and lifetime
is a distinct and often underestimated exercise.

## Analytical procedures

ICH Q14 and Q2(R2) lifecycle. The duplex content method, AM-006, is likely to be the most
difficult to validate and should be started earliest.

## Continued process verification

Define the parameters and attributes trended, the statistical approach, and the action and
alert limit derivation. The PAT installed for process control provides much of this data
for free, which is an additional argument for the at-line chromatography investment.
