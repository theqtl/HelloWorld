# UO-10 — Sterile filtration

> Status: Seeded | Owner: [TBD] | All content **[SEED]**

## 1. Purpose and position
Reduce bioburden in the drug substance to the specified level before lyophilisation or
filling. Feeds UO-11 or UO-12.

## 2. Mechanism
Size-based retention of microorganisms on a 0.22 micron rated membrane.

## 4. Equipment
EQ-100 filtration assembly, EQ-101 receiving vessel, EQ-102 integrity test instrument.

## 5. Consumables, buffers and reagents
Sterilising grade filter, pre-filter if required, flush medium.

## 7. In-process controls and PAT
Pre-use and post-use integrity testing, in-line pressure, pre-filtration bioburden
sampling. Filter validation with the actual product, including adsorption and
extractables and leachables assessment, is required rather than optional.

## 8. Scale-up and scale-down
Scale on filter area per unit volume, confirmed by throughput trials with representative
material.

## 9. Impurity fate
Clears: microorganisms, particulates. Potential loss: product adsorption to membrane, which
must be quantified rather than assumed negligible.

## 10. Failure modes
RSK-100 integrity test failure, RSK-101 product adsorption loss, RSK-102 filter blockage,
RSK-103 extractables.

## 11. Cleaning, changeover and containment
Single-use. Requires appropriate environmental classification for the operation, determined
by whether the drug substance is handled as sterile or as low-bioburden.

## 12. Environmental and utility load
Minimal.

## 13. Open questions
OQ-017, specifically whether drug substance is sterile or low-bioburden, which determines
the classified area requirement and therefore a significant share of facility cost.
