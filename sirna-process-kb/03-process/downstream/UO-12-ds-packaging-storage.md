# UO-12 — Drug substance packaging and storage

> Status: Seeded | Owner: [TBD] | All content **[SEED]**

## 1. Purpose and position
Package released drug substance into its primary container and place it in controlled
storage. Terminal step of the drug substance process.

## 2. Mechanism
Dispensing into qualified primary packaging under controlled environment, sealing, labelling,
and transfer to defined storage conditions.

## 4. Equipment
EQ-120 dispensing station with local protection, EQ-121 sealing, EQ-122 controlled storage
units with monitoring and alarm.

## 5. Consumables, buffers and reagents
Primary container, closure, secondary packaging, desiccant if applicable.

Lyophilised material is hygroscopic, so humidity control during dispensing is a genuine
control rather than a formality.

## 7. In-process controls and PAT
Environmental monitoring, weight verification, container closure integrity testing,
continuous storage temperature monitoring with alarm and excursion procedure.

## 8. Scale-up and scale-down
Scale on number of containers and dispensing throughput.

## 9. Impurity fate
No change expected. Risk of moisture ingress and of environmental contamination.

## 10. Failure modes
RSK-120 moisture uptake during dispensing, RSK-121 container closure integrity failure,
RSK-122 storage excursion, RSK-123 labelling error.

## 11. Cleaning, changeover and containment
Line clearance between products is critical, since a labelling or segregation failure here
is a direct product mix-up risk.

## 12. Environmental and utility load
Cold storage energy and backup power. Backup power for drug substance storage is a facility
requirement worth specifying early.

## 13. Open questions
OQ-019, storage condition and shelf life, which depend on stability data not yet available.
