# UO-02 — Clarification and crude conditioning

> Status: Seeded | Owner: [TBD] | All content **[SEED]**

## 1. Purpose and position
Remove solid support and particulates, and condition the crude stream so that it can be
loaded onto UO-04 without fouling the column. Feeds UO-03.

## 2. Mechanism
Depth or membrane filtration for particulate removal, optionally with dilution or pH
adjustment to set the load condition.

## 4. Equipment
EQ-020 filtration skid, EQ-021 hold vessel. Single-use filter trains are the common
choice at this scale.

## 5. Consumables, buffers and reagents
Depth filter media, 0.45 or 0.2 micron membrane, flush buffer.

## 7. In-process controls and PAT
In-line turbidity and differential pressure. In-line UV at 260 nm on the flush to confirm
product recovery. Product loss to the filter cake is a real and often overlooked yield term.

## 8. Scale-up and scale-down
Scale on filter area per unit mass, confirmed by Vmax or Pmax sizing trials.

## 9. Impurity fate
Clears: particulates, support fines. Passes through: everything dissolved.

## 10. Failure modes
RSK-020 filter blockage, RSK-021 product hold-up loss.

## 11. Cleaning, changeover and containment
Single-use preferred, so changeover is disposal rather than cleaning.

## 12. Environmental and utility load
Solid waste, flush volumes.
