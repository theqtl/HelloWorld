# Prioritised manual reading list

Paywalled or abstract-only sources worth pulling manually, ranked by what they unblock. Full
citations and reachability are in the [source register](../registers/sources.md).

## Highest value

1. **Zhou, Kiesman et al., kg-scale convergent oligo synthesis, J. Org. Chem. 2022 (+SI)** —
   [SRC-ZHOU-2022]. The best worked example of block purities/yields and impurity control at
   fragment vs final stage, at kg scale. Unblocks the purity-floor inputs (Q-011).
   *Paywalled.*
2. **Almac/Alnylam, ACS Chem. Biol. 2023 — Supporting Information** — [SRC-ALMAC-2023].
   Main text has no purity numbers; the SI likely holds ATP concentration, enzyme loading, and
   full byproduct structures. Unblocks ligation conditions (Q-010). *SI access needed.*
3. **Banik, Qian, Mason & Zydney, charged membranes for UF of siRNA, J. Membr. Sci. 2025, 713:123274** —
   [SRC-ZYDNEY-2024]. The only oligo-specific UF source: sieving vs cut-off, charge and
   ionic-strength effects, and concentration from 52 to >180 mg/mL. The full text is the only way to
   settle which cut-offs were tested (Q-035). Unblocks UF/DF design. *Abstract open, full text paywalled.*

## High value

4. **Kumar et al., microbiological control for oligonucleotide DS, OPRD 2025** —
   [SRC-OPRD-2025]. The bioburden and endotoxin ladder on our microbial page is **unverified** and
   reproduces a mammalian cell-culture article almost verbatim. This paper is the only way to
   establish real oligonucleotide limits. Unblocks Q-037 and R-012. *Paywalled — highest-value pull
   on this list after items 1 to 3.*
5. **Banik et al., ligand density and membrane charge for siRNA UF, Sep. Purif. Technol. 2025** —
   [SRC-ZYDNEY-2025]. The sequel to item 3 and the actual home of the ~193 g/L concentration figure
   that was previously misattributed to the 2024 paper. Unblocks Q-017 and the evaporation-duty
   question. *Paywalled.*
6. **Anion-exchange membrane adsorbers for endotoxin removal, PMC12226154** — endotoxin
   clearance and conductivity limits, on **buffer matrices containing no protein and no
   oligonucleotide**. *Open — mine for the oligo-feed gap (R-007).*

## Reference / confirmatory

7. **Nature Communications 2024, template-dependent ligation of modified oligos** — open access;
   ~5% fragment carry-through and 82–96% conversions. Re-read for fidelity detail.
8. **NAR 2025 review, liquid-phase synthesis to chemical ligation** — open access; consolidated
   fragment purity/yield benchmarks.
9. **Vutrisiran (Amvutra) EPAR** — dual orthogonal AX + IPRP HPLC control; numeric DS limits
   redacted (Q-033). *Reachable; specs confidential.*
10. **Patents US10640812 and WO2023164631A1** — ligase modification tolerance and
    high-concentration UF/DF retentate. *Reachable.* (A third patent number previously listed here
    was never verified and has been removed rather than carried unchecked.)
